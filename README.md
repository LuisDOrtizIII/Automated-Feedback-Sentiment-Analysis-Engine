# 📊 Automated Feedback Analysis Workflow
**Tools:** Make.com | Google Gemini 2.5 Flash | Google Forms | JSON Parsing | Google Sheets

## 🚀 Project Overview
[cite_start]This project solves the challenge of manual data entry and inconsistent analysis of customer feedback by providing a fully automated system that instantly processes incoming survey data.  [cite_start]The workflow transforms raw, qualitative data into structured, actionable insights in real-time.

[**Click here to view the Full Project Presentation**](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1tw62SQQXCRzks0qMEK9TTSjiFwEQVz3DHe4w5_WXXuc/edit?usp=sharing)

## 🛠️ Key Technical Features
* [cite_start]**Real-Time Orchestration:** Developed a seamless workflow in **Make.com** that triggers immediately upon new Google Form submissions. 
* [cite_start]**AI Sentiment Intelligence:** Integrated **Google Gemini 2.5 Flash** to perform deep sentiment analysis and distill complex feedback into concise, one-sentence summaries. 
* [cite_start]**Structured Data Transformation:** Implemented a **JSON Parser** and dedicated data structures to translate AI responses into "data bubbles" for accurate system-wide mapping. 
* [cite_start]**Automated Record Management:** Engineered a search-and-update logic to synchronize AI insights directly back to the original entry in Google Sheets. 

## 📈 Impact & Results
* [cite_start]**100% Accuracy:** Successfully classified sentiment for all test cases, including identifying high-dissatisfaction levels in complex technical descriptions. 
* [cite_start]**Operational Speed:** Achieved an average processing time of under 3 seconds per entry, eliminating manual review delays. 
* [cite_start]**Data Integrity:** Maintained 100% consistency in formatting and mapping AI-generated data to centralized business databases. 

## 🔮 Future Enhancements
* [cite_start]**Critical Alert System:** Adding a router to trigger instant Slack or Email notifications for "Negative" sentiment. 
* [cite_start]**Global Scalability:** Integrating a Translation module to analyze multi-language feedback. 
* [cite_start]**Automated Drafts:** Expanding the workflow to generate personalized AI email responses based on specific customer complaints.
